How to play

  1. One person clicks Create room and shares the room code. Everyone else opens the game on their own PC, types the code and clicks Join. Up to 8 players can join, and anyone who joins after that watches as a spectator.
  2. The host picks the genre and rules. The game draws a random pool of players × picks items, and each one has a secret rank.
  3. Everyone starts with £20. Items come up one at a time. The player whose turn it is opens the bidding at £1, and anyone can raise from their own PC. When the timer runs out, or the host presses SOLD, the highest bidder gets the item.
  4. You always keep at least £1 for each empty slot, so you can always fill your team.
  5. Ranked reveal: ranks are revealed from last to first, and better items score more points. Player vote: everyone votes for the best team, but you can't vote for your own.
